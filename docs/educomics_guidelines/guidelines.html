Educational Comic Guidelines

Self-Assessment for Authors, Artists, and Educators

authored by Michaela Topalovic
published September 2025
0% compliance

Development

Analyze the educational context as well as learner knowledge and needs prior to development to guide design choices.

Plan the comic around what learners should know or be able to do after interacting with it.

Develop the comic to be self-sufficient and easily adaptable for various contexts, audiences or educational purposes.

Evaluate and revise the comic regularly based on feedback, research, and evolving educational needs to ensure effectiveness.

Structure and Format

Use simple, authentic, and conversational language that is appropriate for the learner’s level when technical terms are not required.

Keep the comic as well as the text short and focused, and break longer sections into clear, standalone segments.

Provide questions, tasks and activities about the comic’s content that progressively increase in difficulty, accompanied by clear instructions and assessment criteria.

Content

Include content that is accurate, appropriately cited, and legally compliant.

Frame the comic as a narrative with a logical and continuous story progression containing a clear beginning, middle, and conclusion.

Narrate the content through a few expressive and well-defined human or personified characters.

Employ realistic, representative, and relevant examples grounded in real life and industry contexts.

Visual Design

Use a limited and consistent set of fonts and text formatting to support, enhance and stimulate reading.

Apply color purposefully and consistently, keeping the color palette balanced and limited.

Utilize contrast, visual hierarchy and cues to emphasize essential content and guide the learner's focus to it.

Ensure a balanced layout with sufficient white space to avoid clutter and maintain visual harmony.

Place text next to relevant imagery while arranging elements such as panels, captions, and speech balloons carefully to establish a clear reading sequence.

Deployment

Use the comic as a complementary and assistive tool in delivering the content, enhancing retention through effective repetition.

Ensure the comic conveys its real-world value and personal significance to learners, fostering motivation and personal involvement.

Utilize the comic to promote confidence and ease in learners, fostering positive attitudes towards the subject.

Developed as part of a bachelor's thesis at the St. Pölten University of Applied Sciences under the supervision of FH-Prof. Dr. Victor-Adriel De-Jesus-Oliveira, these guidelines were formed through a literature review and validated through an expert evaluation. They provide a structured approach for potential authors, artists and educators who want to create or utilize educational comics, addressing both general principles of educational material design and aspects specific to comics.
